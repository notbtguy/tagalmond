Frequently Asked Questions

Can I balance this cohort with my hectic medical duties?

Yes. MedResearchMate was structured specifically for MBBS students, interns, and residents. All live masterclasses are recorded, and tasks are partitioned into weekly deliverables with direct 24/7 doubt resolution over WhatsApp and mentorship channels.

How does authorship attribution work?

Authorship follows strict ICMJE (International Committee of Medical Journal Editors) guidelines. Every scholar makes substantial contributions to conception, literature screening, statistical extraction, and manuscript review, guaranteeing legitimate co-authorship.

Do I need prior coding or statistical experience in R?

Not at all. We teach you from foundational data entry to running packages like `meta` and `metafor` in R with step-by-step templates and code scripts.
